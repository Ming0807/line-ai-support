# PUB-03 — relationship retrieval and delivery contract audit

Date: 6 October 2026
Status: read-only contract audit; no implementation or acceptance claim.
Owner: `/root/located_chunk_builder` (report only).

## Governing requirements

The current PUB-03 freeze requires family-rule revision evidence to travel through retrieval, saved AI results, citations and dispatch; a newly applicable amendment or cancellation must invalidate an old base answer even while the base chunk remains. Eligibility by date, scope, authority, approval and public visibility precedes similarity. AMENDS/CANCELS instruments are evaluated independently of `is_current`; current and historical answers use their effective intervals. Required amendment context may not be silently dropped when the result cap is reached; the result must become a controlled clarification instead. See [publication plan §PUB-03](../../../docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-yru-import-publication.md) and its lock contract at lines 15–16.

This is consistent with master §39 (version replacement is history-preserving), §40 (an amendment does not supersede the base; retrieve the base and active amendments), and §62 (current/effective, authority, department relevance, similarity, freshness) in [the master guide](../../../CODEX_IMPLEMENTATION_GUIDE_YRU_AI_HELPDESK.md). The original versioning source asks for base plus all active amendments and preserves historical versions ([original document-versioning source](../../../docs/requirements/sources/original-document-versioning.th.md), [§16](../../../docs/requirements/sources/original-document-versioning.th.md)). DEC-025 fixes relation direction as new source document → exact older/base target; AMENDS is direct-to-base only in V1; CANCELS targets one whole document/version and cancellation of an amendment must leave the base and other amendments intact ([import design](../../../docs/architecture/KNOWLEDGE_IMPORT_DESIGN.md)). DEC-025 clarification says AMENDS/CANCELS instruments remain `ACTIVE`, `is_current=false` and do not clear the base's current flag; their approval, scope and dates are evaluated independently ([decision log](../../../docs/decisions/DECISION_LOG.md)). DEC-026 keeps extraction READY/FAILED separate from publication completion, and DEC-028 requires embedding/network work outside business locks ([decision log](../../../docs/decisions/DECISION_LOG.md), [line 55]).

## Current source behavior and gaps

`public.document_families` has no rule revision column today; `documents.revision` is per document. `document_relationships` stores directional source/target/type rows, and the only explicit relationship index is on `target_document_id` ([baseline RAG migration](../../../supabase/migrations/20261004125859_knowledge_rag.sql), lines 43–69). The relationship table has no family, date, scope or approval snapshot; those facts must be joined from its documents and rechecked under the family lock.

`searchKnowledge` materializes eligible documents before chunk similarity. It already filters approval, official source, extraction review, public visibility, review/archive state, family, audience/student scope and effective dates ([retrieval.ts](../../../lib/knowledge/retrieval.ts)). However, the current query has no relationship join or family epoch. Current results require `ACTIVE AND is_current` at lines 46–49, so the new valid `ACTIVE/is_current=false` amendment instruments cannot be included. The historical branch admits `ACTIVE`, `SUPERSEDED` and `EXPIRED` without separating relationship instruments from base versions (lines 50–52). Its `row_number` partitions all admitted rows by family/stream (lines 35–36); with an amendment present, an amendment can win the as-of rank over its base. For a year-only historical request, `scope_ambiguity` counts every eligible row in that family/stream (lines 53–55), so an amendment can be mistaken for a competing document version. Version selection and historical ambiguity must operate on base versions only; relation instruments need their own eligibility path.

There is also a scope edge: the current department predicate at retrieval line 42 accepts every department when `departmentCode` is null, while `scopeIsGrounded` treats a null label as grounded ([answer-producer.ts](../../../lib/knowledge/answer-producer.ts), lines 132–143). Audience/student fields do not behave the same way: a null request only matches `ALL` values through lines 43–45. PUB-03 should freeze department behavior explicitly. Recommended conservative rule: when a department is known, include global documents plus that department; when it is unknown, do not let arbitrary department-specific sources compete as if universally applicable—return a scope clarification if the candidate rule depends on a department. Do the same for an unresolved audience/student dimension when an applicable relationship has a narrower scope. Do not broaden a null scope to “all departments” silently.

The current search orders chunks by authority then similarity (lines 62–67); it does not represent family rule groups, department relevance, or freshness. This should become a deterministic two-stage operation: resolve applicability/group membership first, then score/rank eligible group members. Keep the master’s authority → department relevance → semantic similarity → freshness priority for ranking after current/effective eligibility is resolved.

The query output is only `KnowledgeEvidence[]`, whose evidence includes the chunk/document fields and optional legacy `sourceLocations`, but no family ID, stream, rule revision, as-of snapshot or group membership ([knowledge types](../../../lib/knowledge/types.ts), lines 59–61). `citationEvidenceSchema` is strict and likewise has no family-rule proof; `buildCitedAnswer` copies only citation fields, while `evidenceStillMatches` compares individual cited chunk identity/content/metadata/location and ignores whether the rule family gained or lost an applicable member ([citations.ts](../../../lib/knowledge/citations.ts), lines 48–76). Thus an unchanged cited base row can still compare equal after an amendment is published.

`AIResult` is a strict encrypted `ANSWER` record containing output, scope, up to 12 evidence items, query vector and fingerprint, with no rule snapshot ([jobs.ts](../../../lib/ai/jobs.ts), lines 51–64). `createKnowledgeProducer` searches with limit 12, then truncates evidence to 8 before sending it to the model (answer-producer.ts:252–275). A required group could therefore be complete in SQL yet lose a later amendment from the model context through the `.slice(0,8)`. Either make retrieval return a complete bounded rule-group result already capped at 12 and pass all of it onward, or carry a separate bounded rule snapshot that survives chunk trimming; never cut an amendment while keeping the base answerable.

Worker finalization re-searches and compares only the model-selected `cited` subset while holding `FOR SHARE` locks on cited document rows, then queues a source-change clarification if it detects drift ([run-worker.ts](../../../lib/ai/run-worker.ts)). At dispatch, `verifyAIOutboxEvidence` again reads only selected citations, derives their family IDs, takes family then document session advisory locks, opens a fresh transaction, and compares those cited chunks against `searchKnowledge(..., limit:12)` ([delivery-fence.ts](../../../lib/knowledge/delivery-fence.ts)). Membership/epoch must be checked for every rule group supplied to the model, not only a cited base row; otherwise context the answer used but did not cite can change without invalidation.

## Recommended bounded retrieval/result contract

1. Resolve one temporal selector before ranking. Current uses the Bangkok current date (or the deterministic `today` test seam) and eligible current base rows. An explicit as-of date selects rows whose inclusive effective interval contains that date. A year-only historical request selects exact `academic_year` base versions as master §34 specifies. If a relationship boundary falls within that academic year and there is no exact as-of date to decide whether it applies, clarify instead of inventing a date conversion. Keep the existing ambiguity response for multiple base versions in a family/stream when the selector cannot identify one.

2. Build base candidates per `(family_id, version_stream)` using only non-instrument documents. Apply `APPROVED`, official source, extraction reviewed, `not requires_review`, allowed visibility, non-archive, family, department, audience, student type, semester/program/curriculum/cohort, status/current-version, academic year and effective-date rules before similarity. Current mode chooses eligible `ACTIVE/is_current=true` bases. Historical mode selects the requested version(s) from `ACTIVE/SUPERSEDED/EXPIRED` according to the exact year/date selector; do not rank an instrument as a base version. The current `archive_only=false` predicate applies even to historical mode; keep that behavior explicit unless a separately approved requirement changes it.

3. For each selected base, join direct `AMENDS` sources with that exact target. Independently require the amendment instrument to be approved, official, reviewed, not under review, visible to this retrieval surface, effective at the selector, and applicable to the request's department/audience/student scope. It may be `ACTIVE/is_current=false`; do not require `is_current`. Do not follow AMENDS chains in V1. Evaluate direct `CANCELS` sources the same way, also independent of `is_current`: an effective cancellation of an amendment removes only that exact amendment; cancellation of the base makes the whole rule group unavailable; before cancellation becomes effective, historical as-of retrieval retains the formerly applicable target. Never mutate the base's current flag because a relation instrument exists.

4. Compose the complete `RuleGroup` (`selected base + every applicable direct amendment + the effective cancellation effects`) before calculating similarity. Store a stable group key `(familyId, versionStream, selectedBaseId)` and a deterministic snapshot of member document IDs/revisions and active relation IDs/types/targets. Reject or clarify an unresolved scope when a narrower instrument could apply differently for the unknown audience/department. This makes “base + all active amendments” a set-completeness rule, not a similarity top-k accident.

5. Rank groups/chunks only after the prior eligibility and composition steps. For every selected group, reserve at least one best-scoring non-review chunk for the base and for every applicable amendment. Apply the similarity threshold to these required representatives; if any member has no eligible vector/chunk or no representative meets the threshold, return a controlled clarification/no-evidence outcome rather than an incomplete base-only answer. Then fill remaining slots with other eligible high-scoring chunks using authority, exact-department relevance, similarity, freshness, and deterministic ID tie-breaks. The aggregate result remains at most 12 evidence items; if mandatory member representatives exceed 12, signal context overflow and clarify without returning a partial group. Do not slice the completed group result to 8 in `createKnowledgeProducer`.

6. Add a monotone family `rule_revision` (prefer a canonical decimal string at the TypeScript/JSON boundary if the SQL column is `BIGINT`) and increment it in the same publication transaction whenever publication changes any rule eligibility: replacement/current stream, additional current stream, AMENDS/CANCELS insertion, or a relevant approved/visibility/scope/effective-state change. The epoch update must share the existing family lock and family row lock. Because effective dates change applicability as time passes without a write, also emit a `ruleContextDigest`/snapshot per selected group, bound to the actual `asOfDate` and the sorted selected base/amendment/cancellation IDs+revisions. Compare that digest at finalization and dispatch; the family counter catches publication changes, the snapshot catches date-boundary changes.

7. Carry the selected group proof with every retrieval evidence/citation and in the encrypted AI result: at minimum `familyId`, `versionStream`, `ruleRevision`, `ruleContextDigest`, and the evaluation date. Preserve the proof into stored citation metadata without rendering internal counters to the student. Delivery must lock all families represented in the saved model context, not just `citationChunkIds`, then run a fresh relation-aware search in a new READ COMMITTED statement and compare the saved group snapshots plus cited chunk rows. If any group changed, do not deliver the prior answer.

8. Keep strict backward compatibility explicit. Old encrypted AI results/citations can remain parseable as a `legacy` shape with the original evidence intact, but missing `ruleRevision`/context must never be filled from today's family row. A legacy answer cannot prove its former group membership; suppress its pending delivery or send a fixed non-answer clarification. New results must carry the full rule snapshot. Historical records remain auditable and unmodified; this is not automatic trust promotion.

## Lock and API/test compatibility

`runOutboxCycle` uses a dedicated session-mode client and acquires conversation/delivery session locks before calling the evidence fence (run-outbox.ts:20–35). The fence then acquires sorted family locks and sorted document locks with `pg_advisory_lock`; it starts/commits a READ COMMITTED transaction only for fresh retrieval and releases those session locks in reverse order afterward (delivery-fence.ts:26–40; run-outbox.ts:57–79). The LINE HTTP call therefore runs with session locks held but no open SQL transaction. Publication currently takes the same family/document lock names with `pg_advisory_xact_lock`, after the job lock and family-code lock, before family/target row locks (`import-publication.ts:87–103`). PostgreSQL session and transaction advisory locks on the same hashed key conflict as desired: publication waits for the dispatch's bounded LINE request, or dispatch waits for publication and then re-reads committed rules. Preserve the shared key constructors and `family → sorted documents → rows` order; do not put provider/LINE/network work inside the publication transaction.

The existing dispatch barrier in `tests/database/ai-worker.integration.ts:196–236` proves an xact advisory family lock waits behind a real paused LINE call and checks that the worker has no open transaction. It currently acquires the xact lock directly rather than calling `approveImport`; PUB-03 should retain this primitive regression and add one barrier using the actual publication finalizer/epoch update. Extend the existing source-change, historical ambiguity and location-equality tests in `tests/database/ai-worker.integration.ts:58–101,128–166` and `tests/database/knowledge-locations.integration.ts:10–39` with relationship membership and stale queued base-answer cases.

Required focused cases: base plus two applicable amendments; inactive/future/expired/wrong-audience/wrong-department relation exclusions; history selected by exact year/date without instruments becoming version ambiguity; cancellation of one amendment versus cancellation of the base; historical before and after each cancellation; effective-date boundary between generation and dispatch; overflow at 13 mandatory members; one unusable/missing amendment chunk; family epoch change with the cited base chunk unchanged; unchanged epoch/member digest permits the same cited evidence; legacy missing-epoch result remains readable but is never assigned the current epoch; actual paused dispatch blocks actual publication with no open network transaction. No tests were run for this read-only audit.
