## Dashboard-first operator workflow — DEC-083

The shared Staff OA cannot represent separate per-ticket chat rooms. `/tickets` is the work queue; `/tickets/[id]` is the primary, separately scoped history and composer. Show ticket number and anonymous reporter directly above the composer; key its client state by ticket ID, keep request retries bound to that ID, and place the composer with the conversation. Reading or replying on Dashboard does not switch the independent LINE context. Multiple tabs still execute exact ticket ID/revision/request ID, and concurrent updates require refresh/review rather than retargeting a draft. LINE retains explicit case OPEN and continuous quick replies as selected by the human. Accepting another case or receiving its alert does not switch focus. Receipt copy describes queued work for a named case, not permanent selection or delivery. Full history/private details and reviewed multi-recipient common updates are Dashboard work. AI may suggest drafts/similar incidents; it cannot send personal answers to a group or infer resolution from sending.
