No migration is added by PUB-04.

Pending work closes edits, resets and other mutations; a confirmed receipt suppresses publishing and retains the completed binding. Unknown network outcomes offer receipt recovery or deliberate same-bound retry without claiming the transaction did not commit. STRUCTURED/BOTH remain unavailable until M8.

Source text/title/reason/cells, analyze, save and conflict-resolution controls honor this lock; read/download/navigation remain available after completion.
